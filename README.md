## 🚀 What I'm Building

### SkorKarir — AI Resume Optimizer & ATS Checker

Upload your CV and let AI help you understand exactly where you stand:

| | |
|---|---|
| 📊 | **CV Strength Score** — how competitive is your resume, really? |
| 🤖 | **ATS Compatibility** — will applicant tracking systems even read it? |
| 🔍 | **Gap Analysis** — what's missing that recruiters look for? |
| 🎯 | **Actionable Fixes** — specific, prioritized improvements |
| 💼 | **Career Positioning** — how to present your experience more strongly |

**The flow is simple:**

```
Upload CV  →  AI Review  →  Fix  →  Apply
```

## 🎯 Why SkorKarir?

A lot of people are qualified for the job — their CV just doesn't communicate it well.

SkorKarir exists to close that gap between **who you are** and **what your resume says about you**.

> **Better CV. Better chances. Better career.**
